Everything seeds from the world seed (the WorldCarver random = seed + source-chunk coords) and is deterministic (Pitfall 7). It consumes the Wave-1 carver DATA + the Wave-5 Aquifer (for flooded carving). ZERO new dependencies, ZERO concurrency.
