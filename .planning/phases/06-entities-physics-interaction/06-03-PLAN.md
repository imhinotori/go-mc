## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Untrusted 26.2 client → tick (movement positions) | The client sends positions it claims to be at. A malicious client claims a position inside/through a solid block to clip through walls or fall out of the world. The server collides the claimed position per-axis on the owner goroutine and rejects clip-through. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-6-06 | Tampering | a client claiming a position that tunnels through / sits inside solid blocks | mitigate | collidePlayer collides the client-sent position per-axis against solid block boxes (bvh.AABB.Touch) and clamps it out — the server is authoritative, so a clip-through claim is corrected, not trusted. Asserted by TestNoTunnel + TestPlayerClipRejected. |
| T-6-11 | Tampering | per-axis tunneling of a fast entity through a thin wall | mitigate | moveEntity resolves each axis INDEPENDENTLY against the swept-volume boxes (never full-vector-then-test), so a fast entity cannot pass through a thin wall (Pitfall 4). Asserted by TestNoTunnel. |
| T-6-08 | Tampering/race | physics mutating entity/player/chunk state off the tick goroutine | mitigate | tickPhysics/moveEntity/collidePlayer run on the tick goroutine over tick-owned state (TICK-05); re-bucketing happens on the owner. Proven by the Docker -race gate. |
| T-6-12 | Denial of Service | a huge claimed motion making the swept-volume scan unbounded | accept | The swept volume is bounded by the per-tick motion magnitude; gravity/drag cap an entity's vertical speed, and the player's per-tick delta is bounded by the movement-packet cadence. A pathological claimed delta scans a bounded neighborhood (the entity box ± the clamped delta); it does not scan the world. Hardened movement bounds are post-v1. |
