For v1 the literals are harmless and the reply path may be a documented no-op until Plan 07-05 lands the SystemChat reply helper (which 07-05 can reuse for command output).

This plan is INDEPENDENT of the AI plans (07-01/02/03) — disjoint files — so it runs in Wave 1 in parallel with 07-01.
