## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Untrusted 26.2 client → tick (container clicks) | The client sends ContainerClick with HashedStack digests of what it THINKS the slots hold. A malicious client forges hashes to claim an item it does not have, or sends a malformed/mis-framed click to corrupt the server's inventory or panic the decode. The server owns the inventory and decodes defensively on the owner goroutine. |
