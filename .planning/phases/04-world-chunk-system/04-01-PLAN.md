<objective>
Fix the two confirmed proto-776 wire deviations in `level/chunk.go`, jar-verified against `temp/cache/26.2-server.jar`:

1. **The missing per-section fluid-count short (the confirmed stripes/void bug).** Vanilla `LevelChunkSection.write` writes TWO shorts — `nonEmptyBlockCount` then `fluidCount` — before the states `PalettedContainer`. The fork's `Section.WriteTo` writes only one; `Section.ReadFrom` reads only one (symmetrically wrong). Add a `FluidCount int16` field and write/read both shorts.
2. **The wrong heightmap set.** Vanilla sends only the 3 `sendToClient()` (Usage.CLIENT) heightmaps — WORLD_SURFACE (1), MOTION_BLOCKING (4), MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES (5). The fork's `Chunk.WriteTo` sends all 6. Trim to the 3 CLIENT ids.

Then add a cheap self-round-trip + golden-length regression that locks the two-short layout and the 3-heightmap set natively.

Purpose: These are THE two byte-layout mistakes the research isolated from the jar as the cause of "client renders void or stripes". They are surgical edits to existing, otherwise-correct fork code — `PaletteContainer.WriteTo` (no length prefix, LSB), `heightMapEntry` (typed long array), and `lightData` (field order) are already 776-correct and MUST NOT be rewritten. This plan is the foundation the `world/` package (Plan 04-02) and the streaming/capture-diff (Plans 04-03/04-04) build on; getting the section wire right here is what makes the assembled chunk packet vanilla-correct downstream.
Output: a fixed `level/chunk.go` (Section + Chunk.WriteTo), and `level/chunk_test.go` with the regression tests. ZERO new dependencies.

CRITICAL: the self-round-trip test PASSES on the broken format today (read and write are symmetrically wrong). It is a cheap regression, NOT the correctness proof. The authoritative WORLD-02/03 gate is the vanilla capture-diff in Plan 04-04. Do not treat a green round-trip as "fluid-count is correct".
