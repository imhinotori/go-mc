| T-19-08 | Information disclosure / Tampering | hostile player name/addr in a leave log line corrupting the TUI | mitigate | the slog records flow through Plan 01's formatLine sanitize() before the viewport (control-byte strip) — handled at the single fan-out point, not per-call-site |
| T-19-09 | Spoofing/Repudiation | a drop with no recorded reason hides why a player left | mitigate | the full taxonomy tags every drop site; the leave line always logs a reason (defaulting to "quit"/clean-quit) so no disconnect is unattributed |
