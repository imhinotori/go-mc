The OreVeinifier places copper/iron vein deposits into the solid rock. Together they REPLACE Plan 09-04's provisional fill with the real `doFill` placement.

This plan delivers: (1) `world/levelgen/aquifer.go` — the ported `NoiseBasedAquifer` (`computeSubstance` + the `FluidStatus`/`FluidPicker` + the barrier/fluid-level/lava sampling over the aquifer grid); (2) `world/levelgen/orevein.go` — the ported `OreVeinifier` (`vein_toggle`/`vein_ridged`/`vein_gap` → `VeinType` copper/iron placement); (3) `world/levelgen/fill.go` — the real `doFill` loop consulting both per block (replacing `FillProvisional`).

Everything seeds from the world seed via the Wave-2 positional factory (no `math/rand`) and is deterministic (Pitfall 7). ZERO new dependencies, ZERO concurrency.

The carvers (Wave 6) run on top of this filled terrain; the surface (Wave 7) goes over it; the Generator (Wave 8) drives the whole fill.
